# Animation & Play-Feel Gate: human review

Open the existing `Unreal/DaoVoTan3D.uproject` with UE 5.8.3 and Play StillwaterValley. For real Cultivate, use the existing `Tools/RunCultivationCommandDemo.ps1` launcher and its TypeScript worker.

- WASD moves; mouse looks; Shift toggles walk/run; Space jumps.
- Q toggles camera-facing strafe mode. In this mode move sideways/backwards and rotate the camera while standing to review turn-in-place. Q again returns to facing movement.
- LMB plays the existing cosmetic jian action. E/M requests real Cultivate; +/- changes strategic days. 1 changes the cosmetic aura preset.

Walk from rest, release movement, accelerate to run, reverse direction, pivot left/right, strafe, turn while stationary, jump, walk off a low step, land, attack and Cultivate. Inspect foot contact from the side, knee direction, robe/leg overlap, sword alignment, head/hair attachment and matching portraits. Confirm meditation suppresses movement and returns to locomotion; a duplicate receipt must not grant another session.

Please record camera comfort, start/stop responsiveness, foot sliding, pivots/turns, jump/landing feel and clipping. The robe remains rigid prototype geometry, not simulated cloth. Automated input, pose-selection and receipt tests do not establish human comfort or art acceptance. No additional gameplay systems or Visual Gate 2 work is included.
