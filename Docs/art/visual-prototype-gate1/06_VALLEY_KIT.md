Target reusable kit: 4 rocks, 2 cliffs, 2 trees, 1 grass patch/system, 1 ground material/patch family, 1 gate, 1 altar. Counts are capacity targets, **no meshes/map exist**. Build one quality shape/material language, vary orientation/scale within bounds, reuse distant silhouettes. No arbitrary/generated filler counts as complete kit.

Lighting: one primary directional light/sky, gentle fog/atmosphere and stable exposure; limited accent effects near altar. Sculpt foreground/midground/background separation and a clear playable route. Collision blocks unsafe cliff edges and leaves enough space for camera/sword motions. Terrain slope and foot contact validated after rig locomotion.

World dressing consumes presentation hints only; foliage, fog or loaded mesh does not decide core ecology/resources/time. Local ambience is cosmetic. Spawn player and exactly two NPC proxies from recipe fixtures. NPC idle/turn previews are labeled mock presentation, not autonomous simulation decisions.
