# 05 — Robe, hair and head production decisions

Robe recommendation: **B — skinned upper robe + separate lower panels**. Reconstruct a fitted underbody, upper sleeves/armholes, and front/back/side lower panels with deliberate overlap and leg clearance. Keep the broad design, rebuild the lower garment topology. The raw long skirt joins the entire surface and covers both knees; meditation hip flexion and cross-legged poses cannot be trusted with simple nearest-bone weights. Add thickness/interior only where camera/poses reveal it. Future cloth-compatible layout is possible, but no cloth simulation is introduced.

Hair recommendation: **rebuild as larger controlled clumps**, retaining the topknot/long-back silhouette. It is a broad solid sheet with ragged thin tips and root connections to the same body surface; apparent strands are not separate riggable shells. Static side/back views show proximity to shoulder/upper robe and long reach toward the waist, so torso twist, arm elevation and sword recovery carry clipping risk. Do not claim animated intersections were tested. A few cosmetic chains may be added later after clump reconstruction; no Groom or complex physics.

Head decision: **RETOPO / RESCULPT**. Keep the broad face/profile design. Nose/chin silhouette is recognizable, but eyes/mouth are shallow generated surface forms with no demonstrated mouth cavity, separate eyes, eyelids or expression loops. Ear form is obscured by hair and not separately editable. At the third-person distance the silhouette can work; the existing portraits expose the faceting/soft features, so face and neck deserve cleaner geometry before facial expressions. No facial animation implemented.

Hands are a **REBUILD**, not a smoothing exercise: current finger strips cannot articulate around the existing jian grip. Boots are RETOPO/RESCULPT around Manny foot/ball proportions. Belt/accessory strips can keep their design as OPTIONAL independent attachments after the body is rebuilt.
