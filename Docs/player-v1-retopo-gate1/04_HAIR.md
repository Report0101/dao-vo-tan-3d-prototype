# Hair

One skeletal Hair module contains ten deliberate shells: cap, topknot, two controlled front strands and six broad back clumps. No AI sliver topology, Groom, physics or extra cosmetic bones. Initial weights blend head/spine_05 for lower back clumps; cap/front/knot follow head.

Hair is separable from body and remains outside most back volume in the captured meditation view; that is not certification of all animated intersections. Root/cap fit and rigid clump motion need silhouette cleanup. Hair has 9,136 triangles, above the 4–7k group target; cap/clump subdivision is obvious reducible waste. Optional future 2–4 cosmetic chains may follow head/neck/spine after clearances are solved, without a second gameplay skeleton.
